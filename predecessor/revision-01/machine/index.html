Consensus grammar

Eleven movements. No silent conversion.

01 · BOUND

Open one temporary locus.

No participant or relation is created.

02 · PRESENT_FORM

Expose bounded identity.

Presentation is necessary and never sufficient for admission.

03 · GATE_DISPOSITION

Answer the presentation.

ADMIT, REFUSE, WITHHOLD, or HOLD.

04 · ENTER

Create attributable presence.

Entry remains separate from Matter admission.

05 · OBSERVE_CROSSING

Record that Matter crossed.

Observation does not prove truth or uptake.

06 · MATTER_DISPOSITION

Set one locality’s posture.

No actor can set another locality’s disposition.

07 · RECORD_EMERGENCE

Commit a field-local delta.

Every contributor must be present and have admitted named Matter.

08 · CORRECT

Append a correction edge.

The predecessor remains visible.

09 · EXIT

End one actor’s presence.

The locus and its trace remain.

10 · CLOSE

End the locus.

Addressed residue is produced without automatic incorporation.

11 · INCORPORATE…

Let an addressee decide locally.

No source field is mutated and no external uptake is inferred.
