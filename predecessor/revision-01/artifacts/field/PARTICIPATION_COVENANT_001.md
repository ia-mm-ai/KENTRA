# PARTICIPATION_COVENANT_001

Status: **PUBLIC COVENANT · OPERATIVE ONLY WHEN AN EXACT LIVE FIELD BINDS IT**

This covenant states the minimum encounter conditions the KENTRA surface is built
to preserve. Reading or copying it does not accept it on behalf of any person or
locality.

## 1. Capacity precedes invitation

The Host declares situated capacity before opening presentation. When capacity is
unavailable, uncertain, or exceeded, the Gate closes or uses `HOLD`. Demand does
not silently widen capacity.

## 2. Presentation is not admission

A presented Form becomes inspectable. Presentation creates neither participation,
Authority, entry, nor relation. The presenting locality remains distinct.

## 3. Gate disposition is exact

The Host may answer a current presentation with `ADMIT`, `REFUSE`, `WITHHOLD`, or
`HOLD`. The answer applies only at its recorded scope. `ADMIT` creates eligibility
to enter; it is not entry.

## 4. Entry is attributable

Only the admitted actor's valid act creates its Presence. A browser request,
delivery receipt, wallet connection, signature prompt, or Host preference cannot
substitute for that act.

## 5. Matter remains locally answerable

Crossing is recorded before interpretation. Every present locality may separately
`ADMIT`, `REFUSE`, `WITHHOLD`, or `HOLD` exact Matter for itself. No participant may
set another locality's posture.

## 6. Interior state is not required

Private state is never required on chain. A participant exposes only the bounded
public commitments it deliberately submits. Public operations and receipts remain
public consensus data when a live chain records them.

## 7. Correction appends

A correction preserves its predecessor and adds an attributable edge. It does not
erase what occurred.

## 8. Exit remains possible

A present actor may end its Presence. Exit does not erase the encounter, close the
field, or force uptake of any residue.

## 9. Closure does not incorporate

Closure ends the locus and may create separately addressed residue. Only the
addressed locality can later decide whether to incorporate that residue locally.

## 10. No automatic force

Conformance, publication, availability, presentation, successful execution, or
shared ancestry creates no external jurisdiction, adoption, Source relation,
Authority transfer, identity continuity, or shared currentness by itself.
